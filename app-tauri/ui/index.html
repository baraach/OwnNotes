Welcome to OwnNotes

Your notes are files in a folder you own. Three things worth knowing:

  1. Write. Press ⌘N, type, and it saves itself.
  2. Sync. Point Settings → Sync & Storage at any WebDAV folder — Nextcloud, Koofr, a NAS. Nothing leaves your control.
  3. Find. ⌘K jumps to a note, ⌘F searches inside one.
